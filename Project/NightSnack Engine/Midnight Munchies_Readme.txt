## Game Concept
## --------------------------
Title: SoulBound
Genre: 2D Platformer Puzzle
Core Idea:
Our game is a 2D side-scrolling platform adventure game. 
The core identity of the game is its unique Possession mechanic. 
Raincharm can possess enemies and then take on their forms and abilities.


## Player Controls
## --------------------------
A Key: Moves player left
D Key: Moves player right
Spacebar Key: Player jump / ability

W Key: Move up when Frog Tongue is attached to ceiling
S Key: Move down when Frog Tongue is attached to ceiling

J Key: Possess/Unpossess statue monsters

Abilities: 
Hold Spacebar Key(when in air): Ghost|Glide, HellFrog|Tongue, 
Spacebar Key(when in air): FlyEye|Extra Jump


## Debug Controls
## --------------------------
O: Activate debug-drawing
F1: Display FPS
F2: Default Dialogue display
Escape: Toggle Pausing Game Scene


## Step by Step Physics debug
## --------------------------
Ctrl + P: Toggle Step by Step Module
P: When enabled, Step to next frame


## How To Play
## --------------------------
Start the game from [Main Menu > Start]
Complete Tutorial to get a feel of the main controls
Progress to Level 1, 2 and final level 3
Explore the environment to find the blue flames to unlock and exit door
Use abilities like possession, Grappling, double jump and slingshot launch to overcome obstacles
Complete the goal: reach exit
